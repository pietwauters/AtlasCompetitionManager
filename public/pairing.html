Pair a scoresheet

Each device gets its own unique credential — assigning one picks the next unused entry from the pool, no code to type in. Walk to the new device, tap "Assign a credential" below, then let them scan the QR with their camera (opens the e-scoresheet app already paired), or read the username/password aloud for manual entry. Dismiss this screen once the device has it — the credential doesn't expire on its own the way a short-lived code would.
